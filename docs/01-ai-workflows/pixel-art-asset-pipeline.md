## What It Is & Why It Matters

Prompting a standard image diffusion model for "pixel art" almost always yields unusable game assets: inconsistent pixel sizes ("mixels"), anti-aliased muddy halos around transparent edges, wildly clashing color temperatures between characters and tilesets, and walk cycles whose feet drift off-grid. On September 7, 2026, while building *PocketRPG Studio* (`D:\Projects\RPG Projects`), we engineered a deterministic local ComfyUI pixel-art factory that solves grid alignment, silhouette readability, and cross-pack palette cohesion simultaneously.

Instead of generating sprites from pure random noise, the pipeline starts by ingesting clean public-domain (CC0) pixel-art silhouettes from OpenGameArt and Kenney (`Liberated Pixel Cup`, `Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup`, and `0x72` pools). Those base shapes feed a **Silhouette-Guided Img2Img** pass at `0.55–0.65` denoise—high enough to invent completely new armor, mechanical plating, and surface detail, yet tight enough to preserve readable anatomy, weapon stances, and tile borders.

To keep hundreds of sprites looking like they belong to the same commercial game, we paired **Dual-Conditioning** (IP-Adapter visual style transfer + ControlNet structural edge lock) with our **Standardized 2D Asset CLIP Text Injection Directive**—anchoring every prompt in a pack to explicit material and lighting tokens (such as brass-and-slate steampunk or cyberpunk neon). Post-processing then enforces integer nearest-neighbor downscaling, `16`- or `32`-color indexed palette quantization, clean alpha matting, and automated grid/contour slicing.

## Real Example: How It Works in Practice

- **Input**: A `32x32` CC0 heavy-armor boss silhouette plus a batch request for a `256px` *FF6 Steampunk* mech boss sprite (`boss-mech.png`) and matching parallax battle arena backdrop (`arena-cyberpunk.png`).
- **What Happens**:
  1. **Style & Structure Conditioning**: Python batch scripts submit a JSON workflow payload to ComfyUI (`http://127.0.0.1:8188/prompt`) combining the upscaled silhouette (`0.60` denoise), ControlNet structural lock, IP-Adapter era reference, and our standardized brass-and-slate CLIP style anchor.
  2. **Pixel Grid & Palette Snap**: Once ComfyUI finishes latent decoding, Python post-processors strip background alpha fringes, apply strict nearest-neighbor integer downsampling so every pixel sits on a crisp `1:1` grid, and quantize colors to a cohesive `32`-color ramp.
  3. **Spritesheet & Wang Tile Packaging**: Character sheets are aligned into 8-direction walk grids (`hero_8dir.png`), while terrain textures are cut into `47`-tile Wang blob autotile templates ready for touch painting inside Godot 4.7.
- **Output**: Clean, zero-mixel `.png` game assets written directly into `D:\Projects\RPG Projects\assets\packs\` (`clockwork_ruins`, `cyberpunk_neon`, `cosmic_horror`, `eldritch_abyss`, and `gothic_fantasy`), immediately playable on desktop and Galaxy S24 Ultra Android builds.

## Where It Stands & Next Steps

The pipeline is shipped as a reusable studio workflow (`1,981 LOC` across 13 Python batch/ComfyUI tools inside `D:\Projects\RPG Projects` plus BFS alpha-contour sprite slicing ported into *Aether Engine*). ## Under the Hood & Local Files

### Core Technical Directives (From Unified Memory Bank `M0614–M0631`)
- **Silhouette-Guided Denoise Window (`0.55–0.65`)**: Below `0.50`, output stays too close to the CC0 source template; above `0.70`, diffusion breaks limb proportions and tileable seam edges.
- **AA/AAA 5-Style Studio Matrix (`M0630–M0631`)**: Pre-calibrated Dual-Conditioning recipes for five distinct retro/modern eras, combining IP-Adapter Plus weight balancing with pixel-grid post-quantization.
- **Automated Slicing & Bundling**: Pairs fixed-cell grid slicers (for 8-directional character sheets and `47`-tile Wang autotiles) with Breadth-First Search (BFS) connected-component alpha slicers for irregular prop sheets.
