## What It Is & Why It Matters

*The Belt* is a complete 100-question architectural blueprint—and our studio's earliest multi-module prototype—for a first-person solarpunk and ecosystem-horror immersive sim where a lone Scavenger descends through a decaying subterranean vertical megastructure without weapons, pause-screen inventory grids, dialogue trees, or XP skill menus. Across a seed-generated `2–4 hour` run spanning four interconnected vertical rings (from sunlit *Canopy* vents down through *Toxic Bog*, *Precursor Ruin*, and *Molten Trench* into the flooded *Root Sump*), survival and repair depend entirely on manipulating heat, water flow, acoustic echoes, invasive *Mega-Flora*, and living biological tools strapped physically to your rig.

We designed *The Belt* on day two of our studio (`2026-08-17`) to pioneer our **100-Question `/grill-me` Architectural Interrogation** methodology: before writing a single scene file, we locked `100` granular engineering, rendering, audio, UI, and systemic simulation decisions directly into our LanceDB Unified Memory Bank (`137` stored memories `M0009`–`M0142`, `M0149`–`M0151`). That upfront discipline proved how an immersive sim can generate tension and replayability purely from interacting physical and ecological rules rather than scripted combat arenas.

Even after we retired its initial prototyping host (Summer Engine) in favor of pure Godot 4.7, the architectural patterns born in *The Belt*—decoupled event buses, physical raycast item grabbing, diegetic visor shaders, and deterministic world-state consequences—became the direct blueprint for *Kaiju Kiosk*, *MechDestroyer*, and *Babylon: Final Days of Adubar*.

## Real Example: How It Works in Practice

- **Input**: Descending into a choked Precursor Ruin shaft with an Apex Leviathan patrolling the acoustic dark below, you unclip a starving bioluminescent **Living Grapple-Symbiote** from your physical backpack harness, feed it a harvested spore pod, and carve a drainage channel through a `VoxelTerrain` retaining wall above a thermal vent.
- **What Happens**: Unclipping the heavy canister from your rig immediately lowers carried mass, aerodynamic drag, and metallic walking rattle on the **Acoustic Propagation Grid**, reducing the radius at which the blind Leviathan tracks your footsteps. Feeding the Living Tool mutates its tendril reach instead of letting it wither and die on your belt. Carving the voxel retaining wall releases pooled water onto the superheated vent below; `EcoSystemBus` updates the **Thermodynamic & Fluid Grid**, flashing scalding steam upward to scald invasive Mega-Flora choking a precursor relay pylon while masking your thermal and acoustic signature long enough to slip down into the Root Sump.
- **Output**: If you restore pressure and nutrient balance—or accidentally trigger a runaway thermal cascade—the run concludes without a single line of exposition: a silent, post-credits environmental timelapse fast-forwards decades across all four rings, showing lush symbiotic recovery or total biological collapse caused directly by the physical state you left behind.

## Under the Hood & Local Files

### Key Systems Locked in the 137-Memory Specification
Pulling directly from Unified Memory Bank cluster `M0009`–`M0151`:
- **Zero-Menu Physical Rig & Acoustic Rattle**: Replaces grid inventories with physical attachment sockets on the Scavenger's frame. Overloading sockets slows climbing speed, pulls center-of-mass off-balance on narrow ledges, and emits clanking audio impulses every footstep.
- **Symbiotic Living Tools**: Tools are biological organisms rather than indestructible wrenches—each requires specific substrates, adapts morphologically to repeated environmental exposure, and dies permanently if neglected or exposed to lethal radiation/heat.
- **Coupled Thermodynamic, Fluid & Food-Chain Grids**: `EcoSystemBus` ticks coarse-grained cellular automata for heat, moisture, spores, and sound alongside a three-tier ecology (invasive Mega-Flora, Opportunistic Scavengers, and the territorial Apex Leviathan) so luring a predator with carrion or steam is always a systemic interaction rather than a scripted cutscene.
- **Locked Visual & Platform Standards**: Cel-shaded ligne-claire ink aesthetic inspired by Moebius and *Nausicaä*, paired with subtle visor condensation (`Q44`), fluid container responsive UI scaling (`Q49` supporting `1280x800` Steam Deck presets), and transparent community roadmap milestones (`Q94`).
