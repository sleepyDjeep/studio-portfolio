## What It Is & Why It Matters

Most skilled woodcarvers fall into the hobbyist labor trap: spending 20 hours roughing out a single soft-basswood sculpture by hand, selling it for $300, and earning less than $15 per hour for indoor shelf decor that rots if placed on a porch. We engineered the **Artisan Timber Co.** blueprint on October 5, 2026 to prove how disciplined manufacturing cycle-time splits and marine-grade exterior durability turn traditional hand-carved wood art into a **$65 to $115 per hour** micro-manufactory.

The strategy pairs two structural moats. First, a **four-stage hybrid production workflow** uses bandsaw profile jigs and carbide power burrs to remove 60% of bulk waste wood in 35 minutes, reserving hand knives, palm gouges, and multi-layer milk-paint/acrylic washes strictly for the final 90 minutes where human tool marks create gallery pricing power. Second, switching from indoor basswood to rot-resistant **Northern White Cedar, Bald Cypress, and White Oak** sealed with penetrating marine epoxy and three coats of UV-inhibiting spar urethane opens high-spending outdoor markets—working floating waterfowl decoys, pond otters, brick-wall *Green Man* reliefs, and heirloom patio guardians—where fragile store-bought resin cracks after one season.

To make unit economics tangible before buying timber, we compiled the entire venture plan, six-SKU structural anatomy diagrams, buyer psychology profiles, 30-day launch calendar, and live monthly cash-flow model into a self-contained **8-slide interactive HTML presentation deck**.

## Real Example: How It Works in Practice

- **Input**: Adjusting the three monthly production sliders on **Slide 6 (Interactive Profit & Yield Simulator)** to a balanced part-time workshop batch: **4 Flagship Gunner Decoys**, **12 Mid-Tier Heritage Garden Gnomes / Green Man Plaques**, and **25 Entry-Level Copper-Staked Mushrooms / Folk Art Ice Fish**.
- **What Happens**:
  1. In the shop workflow (Slide 2), cardboard 1:1 silhouette jigs guide **10-minute bandsaw profile pre-cuts**, followed by **25 minutes of rotary carbide roughing**, **45 minutes of hand-gouge facial and feather detailing**, and **45 minutes of layered washes plus marine spar-urethane sealing** (with poured self-righting lead keels bolted onto floating decoys).
  2. Inside the browser deck (`updateCalculator()`), the live ROI engine multiplies each tier's retail average ($385 flagship, $215 mid-tier, $55 entry), deducts exact timber/epoxy/lead-keel consumables ($25, $16, and $5 per unit), and sums hybrid bench hours (4.0 hrs, 2.2 hrs, and 0.6 hrs).
  3. Across the **Three-Tier Profit Pyramid** (Slide 4), the 25 entry stakes ($35–$95) pay off weekend market booth fees inside the first two hours and capture impulse foot traffic; the 12 mid-tier pieces ($140–$340) supply recurring weekly breadwinner cash; and the 4 flagship decoys ($350–$950+) anchor booth authority and deliver 45% of gross profit on just 15% of unit volume.
- **Output**: **$5,495/month in gross sales**, **$417 in material costs**, and **$5,078/month in net income** across **57.4 total shop hours** (~13.3 hours/week)—yielding an effective **$88.46 / hour** labor return with **$0 paid ad spend**.

## Where It Stands & Next Steps

Concrete 30-day execution steps mapped out on Slide 8 when ready to launch:
- **Week 1 (Timber & Jig Tooling)**: Source seasoned Northern White Cedar and Bald Cypress blocks, cut 1:1 rigid profile templates for mallard/wood-duck bodies and gnome silhouettes, and hook dust extraction to the bandsaw and rotary burr station.
- **Week 2 (12-Piece Validation Batch)**: Batch-cut 2 flagship working decoys, 4 expressive 14"–20" garden gnomes, 2 high-relief *Green Man* oak/cypress plaques, and 4 three-packs of copper-rod mushroom stakes while filming overhead roughing-to-carving timelapses.
- **Week 3 (Marine Sealing & Tank Float Tests)**: Apply antiqued acrylic/milk-paint washes, brush 3 coats of UV marine spar urethane, ballast the decoy lead keels in a water test tank to verify instant self-righting, and record water-drop reels.
- **Week 4 (Zero-CAC Pop-Up & Batch Drop)**: Carve live at an affluent regional farmers market or waterfowl expo while releasing the filmed 12-piece batch drop on short-form video channels and pitching 60/40 consignment displays to independent garden nurseries.
