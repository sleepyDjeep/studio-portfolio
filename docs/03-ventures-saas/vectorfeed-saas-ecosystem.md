## What It Is & Why It Matters

County public records sit locked inside thousands of incompatible clerk, assessor, and sheriff portals across the United States. Institutional real-estate data vendors charge $400 to $800 per month for aggregated access, pricing out independent investors, probate attorneys, tax-appeal consultants, and municipal contractors. We built the **VectorFeedHQ** suite to collect, normalize, and verify public filings directly from the primary government source, then sell targeted subscriptions and perpetual tools at $25 to $199.

Instead of cramming every buyer persona into one bloated dashboard, we engineered one shared nationwide ingestion spine and mounted **seven specialized SaaS products** on top of it:

1. **PropertyIntel** (`property.vectorfeedhq.com` — $25/$40/$99 MRR): Tax-deed auctions, sheriff foreclosures, code liens, and Secretary of State LLC officer unmasking, paired with 3,195 programmatic SEO county and state pages.
2. **ProbateIntel** (`probate.vectorfeedhq.com` — $49/$69/$99 MRR): A 50-state statutory creditor bar-clock countdown radar that calculates net estate equity, skip-traces executors, and compiles ReportLab PDF estate dossiers.
3. **GovBid Radar** (`govbid.vectorfeedhq.com`): Municipal and county procurement, RFP, and surplus contract surveillance.
4. **TaxAppeal Pro** (`taxappeal.vectorfeedhq.com` — $79/$199 perpetual, local port `:8160`): A dual-track appraisal district market-comp and statutory uniformity evaluator (§ 41.43 / § 42.26) that batch-compiles 10 to 500 parcel protest briefs (`Exhibits A–C`) in Excel and PDF.
5. **ScrapeForge Desktop** (`scrapeforge.vectorfeedhq.com` — $149 perpetual, local port `:8150`): A standalone local extraction workbench preloaded with 8 government vendor driver presets and an offline HMAC SHA-256 license verifier.
6. **SurplusFinder.io** (`surplusfinder.vectorfeedhq.com` — free search + $29 Claim Kit): A tax-sale overbid lookup portal and state-notarized claim packet generator serving as an organic lead magnet.
7. **APNLookup.org** (`apnlookup.vectorfeedhq.com` — free directory + $29/mo API): A 3,244-jurisdiction parcel, GIS, and county assessor resolver.

This architecture proved a repeatable studio pattern. Write the hard data-normalization and cryptographic provenance engine once, share billing and licensing through a common library, and ship focused storefronts on Cloudflare Pages in days rather than quarters.

## Real Example: How It Works in Practice

- **Input**: Raw multi-state county auction calendars, clerk probate dockets, and tax-lien rolls totaling **15,775 raw listings** pulled across 3,143 US counties and 8 auction software vendors, plus parcel identifiers owned by anonymous holding LLCs.
- **What Happens**:
  1. `CountyFleetOrchestrator` routes each jurisdiction to one of 8 specialized vendor drivers (`TylerOdyssey`, `GranicusLandmark`, `KofileQuickSearch`, `BISTrueRoll`, `RealAuctionGovEase`, `CivicPlusMunicode`, `StateJudicialGateway`, or `ArcGISGIS`), stamping every raw HTML/JSON payload with a **SHA-256 provenance hash** inside a SQLite WAL cache.
  2. If a county portal returns a disguised HTTP 200 CAPTCHA wall ("Silent-200"), the fleet quarantines that node and trips a per-county circuit breaker rather than polluting downstream tables.
  3. QA and deduplication agents normalize street addresses to **USPS Publication 28** standards, collapsing the 15,775 raw rows into **14,904 unique verified parcels**.
  4. The OSINT skip-trace agent queries Secretary of State corporate registries to unmask registered agents and managing members behind anonymous LLC owners, then computes opening-bid-to-market-value equity spreads.
  5. Listings exceeding a 50% equity discount (`25` VIP deals in the initial nationwide batch) trigger instant webhook dispatches, while static site generators render localized county landing pages and interactive redemption calculators.
- **Output**: A formatted **2.23 MB** multi-tab institutional workbook (`output/property_intelligence_master.xlsx`), PDF estate and tax-appeal packets, **5 rendered 9:16 promo video shorts** (`short_houston_tx_92pct.mp4` plus Atlanta, Chicago, Miami, and Phoenix), and **3,195 static SEO HTML pages** indexed under `sitemap.xml`.

## Under the Hood & Local Files

### Fleet Architecture & Key Engineering Decisions

Pulling public records across **3,244 US jurisdictions** (all 3,143 counties and county-equivalents across the 50 states + DC, plus 100 municipios and island districts across Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands) looks impossible if treated as 3,244 custom scrapers. During architectural discovery, we mapped almost the entire country onto **8 recurring municipal software vendors**:

| Vendor Driver | Jurisdictions Covered | Target Portal Family |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| `TylerOdyssey` | 950 | Tyler Technologies Eagle / Odyssey / iasWorld clerk & tax portals |
| `GranicusLandmark` | 620 | Granicus / LandmarkWeb / AcclaimWeb official records |
| `KofileQuickSearch` | 480 | Kofile / GovOS county clerk land & probate indexes |
| `BISTrueRoll` | 350 | BIS Consulting / TrueRoll / Aumentum appraisal rolls |
| `RealAuctionGovEase` | 320 | Realauction, Bid4Assets, GovEase & GrantStreet tax-sale platforms |
| `CivicPlusMunicode` | 280 | CivicPlus municipal RFP, code-lien & procurement boards |
| `StateJudicialGateway` | Statewide | Centralized state court & probate docket aggregators |
| `ArcGISGIS` | Fallback / Parcel | County ArcGIS REST FeatureServers for parcel geometry & APN resolution |

Three Memory Bank decisions shaped the monorepo:
- **Shared Commercial Core (`products/common/`)**: Instead of duplicating authentication and billing across seven apps, every product inherits `base_engine.py`, `stripe_gateway.py`, and an offline-capable HMAC SHA-256 `license_engine.py` for perpetual desktop/protest tools.
- **Maven-Style Light Analytical UI**: Early dark-mode prototypes felt like developer tools rather than institutional financial research. We redesigned `PropertyIntel` around a high-contrast light analytical layout with tabular numerals, clear competitor price comparison matrices ($25/$40/$99 vs. $400–$800 legacy data terminals), and three zero-signup interactive tools (Statutory Redemption Calculator, County Assessor Finder, and Wholesale MAO Calculator).
- **Cryptographic Auditability**: Every scraped parcel stores the raw upstream response hash alongside normalized fields so institutional buyers and tax-protest boards can verify exact county-source lineage.
